Account Setup

👤 How to Sign Up / Save Delivery Details

Set up your delivery profile once for rapid 1-click ordering across all foodZILLA pages.

4 Simple Steps
01

Click the "Sign in" Button

Locate the Sign in button in the top navigation bar at the upper-right corner of the screen.

Top Nav Bar 🦖 foodZILLA ➔ [Sign in] ➔ 🛒 Cart
02

Fill in Your Delivery Information

A popup modal will appear asking for your essential delivery credentials:

  • Name: Your first and last name (e.g. Basem Ahmed).
  • Governorate: Your governorate in Egypt (e.g. Giza or Cairo).
  • City: Your district or municipality (e.g. Mohandessin, Dokki, Nasr City).
  • Address: Your exact street name, building number, and apartment.
  • Phone Number: A valid mobile number (10 to 15 digits) so the delivery driver can reach you.
03

Create Your Password (8 – 16 Characters)

Enter a secure password between 8 and 16 characters. You can click the 👁️ Eye Icon on the right side of the password box anytime to reveal or hide what you typed.

💡
Password Rule: Passwords shorter than 8 characters will show a friendly warning. Keep it between 8 and 16 characters.
04

Click "Save details" & You're Done!

Click the orange Save details button. Your details are now saved in your browser's local memory. The top navigation button will instantly transform from "Sign in" to your personal name!

⚡
Instant Address Sync: Your saved address automatically populates into your shopping cart drawer whenever you place an order. You can edit it anytime by clicking "Edit" inside the cart.
Placing Orders

🛒 How to Order (2 Easy Ways)

Order directly through the online smart cart, or speak to a live operator via our nationwide 24/7 hotline.

Online Cart or Hotline

💻 Method A: Online Shopping Cart

Fastest for customized orders & digital checkout:

  • Browse Recipes: Scroll through the recipes grid on the Home page or the Express Menu on the Contact page.
  • Click "Add to Cart": Each meal has an orange button. You'll see a confirmation alert and the cart counter will increment.
  • Open Cart Drawer: Click the 🛒 Cart button in the top navigation bar to slide open your order summary.
  • Adjust Quantities: Use the + and - buttons to change portions, or click × to remove an item.
  • Click "Buy Now": If signed in, your order is placed immediately! If not, the sign-in modal opens for quick address entry.

📞 Method B: 24/7 Hotline & WhatsApp

Ideal for instant orders over the phone or text:

  • Call Hotline 19844: Click the glowing red pill in the header or dial 19844 on your phone keypad.
  • Average Pickup < 30s: An operator will take your order and assign it to the nearest central kitchen.
  • WhatsApp Ordering: Go to the Contact Page and tap "💬 WhatsApp Order" to send your meal choice directly to our team.
  • Cash or Card at Doorstep: Pay by cash, mobile POS card reader, or InstaPay upon delivery.
📞 Dial Hotline 19844
⚡

Pro-Tip: "Buy Now" Instant Order Button

Want a single meal right away? On any recipe modal or card, click the orange "Buy Now" button. This automatically adds the dish and opens your cart drawer in a single click, ready for immediate checkout!

Culinary Exploration

🔍 How to Find Recipes & Cooking Videos

FoodZILLA is more than just a restaurant — it's a full healthy recipe encyclopedia with nutritional data.

Search & Filters
01

Filter by Nutrition Category

Use the category filter buttons on the Home page to explore meals tailored to your dietary goals:

Categories 🍗 Protein • 🥖 Carbohydrates • 🍰 Sweets • 🍎 Fruits • 🧀 Dairy
02

Instant Dual-Search (Recipe Name or Ingredients)

Type into the search bar at the top of the recipes grid. Our instant search engine checks both the name of the dish and any ingredient simultaneously! For example, typing "avocado" or "salmon" will instantly filter matching recipes in real time.

03

Open the Recipe Modal & Watch YouTube Video

Click anywhere on a recipe card to launch the comprehensive recipe modal. Here you can discover:

  • Exact Calorie Count: Total kilocalories (kcal) per serving.
  • Complete Ingredients: Bulleted list of fresh healthy items.
  • Cooking Steps: Step-by-step instructions to recreate the dish at home.
  • YouTube Video: Embedded video tutorial with full controls.
